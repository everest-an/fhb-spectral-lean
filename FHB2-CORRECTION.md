# FHB-2 · correctness review of the "matchgate" claim

**Status: the claim as stated does not hold.**  Neither generator of the
`RZZ`/`RX` brickwork is a matchgate.  A `RXX`/`RZ` brickwork is.

> **Correction history.**  The first version of this note said "`RZZ` is a
> matchgate; only `RX` is not".  That was wrong.  `Z_l Z_{l+1}` is a *quartic*
> Majorana monomial, so `RZZ` fails the same test as `RX` — for a different
> reason.  Both failures are now witnessed in
> [`fhb2-matchgate-lean`](https://github.com/everest-an/fhb2-matchgate-lean).

## The claim under review

`AwareLiquid/The-Fuxi-Hypercube-Math` describes FHB-2 as

> "a nearest-neighbor **RZZ/RX** brickwork on a line — a **matchgate
> (free-fermion) circuit** whose ⟨Xⱼ⟩ / ⟨XⱼXₖ⟩ references are exactly computable
> in O(n²·d) via Majorana covariance evolution, at any depth and any n."

The covariance route needs every generator to act on Majorana operators as an
**orthogonal** transformation `γ_μ ↦ Σ Q_{νμ} γ_ν`.  That holds exactly when the
generator is at most **quadratic** in the Majoranas.

## Why neither generator qualifies

Jordan–Wigner (indices from 1): `γ_{2l−1} = (∏_{i<l} Z_i) X_l`,
`γ_{2l} = (∏_{i<l} Z_i) Y_l`.

| generator | Majorana form | degree | Gaussian? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Z_l` | `−i γ_{2l−1} γ_{2l}` | 2 | **yes** |
| `X_l X_{l+1}` | `−i γ_{2l} γ_{2l+1}` | 2 | **yes** |
| `Y_l Y_{l+1}`, `X_l Y_{l+1}`, `Y_l X_{l+1}` | quadratic | 2 | **yes** |
| `X_1` | `γ_1` | 1 | yes, after a `(2n+1)` extension |
| `X_l`, `l ≥ 2` | `−i γ_1 γ_2 … γ_{2l−1}` | `2l−1` | **no** |
| **`Z_l Z_{l+1}`** | **`−γ_{2l−1} γ_{2l} γ_{2l+1} γ_{2l+2}`** | **4** | **no** |

The last row is the one the first draft of this note missed.  It is witnessed in
Lean on two qubits:

```lean
theorem ZZ_quartic     : Z1 * Z2 = -(g 0 * g 1 * g 2 * g 3)    -- degree 4
theorem ZZ_conj_cubic  : g 0 * (Z1 * Z2) = -(g 1 * g 2 * g 3)  -- degree 3
```

`ZZ_conj_cubic` is the decisive line: conjugating `γ_1` by `exp(−iθ Z₁Z₂/2)`
produces a cubic component, so the adjoint action is **not** linear and no
covariance matrix can represent it.

Note that `Z_lZ_{l+1}` *is* a matchgate as a **group element** (it is
`−exp(iπ/2 Z_l) · exp(iπ/2 Z_{l+1})`), but it is not in the matchgate **Lie
algebra**, so `exp(−iθ Z_lZ_{l+1})` for general `θ` is not a matchgate.

## What the literature says

* **Wan, Huggins, Lee, Babbush**, *Matchgate Shadows for Fermionic Quantum
  Simulation*, Comm. Math. Phys. (2023):
  > "Matchgates are a particular class of two-qubit gates, generated by two-qubit
  > X rotations of the form `exp(iθ X⊗X)` and **single qubit Z rotations**
  > `exp(iθ Z⊗I)` and `exp(iθ I⊗Z)` … along with the Pauli **X** operator `Xₙ`
  > **on the last qubit**."

  `ZZ` is absent from the generator list, and the last-qubit `Xₙ` is the
  reflection lifting `SO(2n)` to `O(2n)`, not a general `X` rotation.
* **Matchgate synthesis** (arXiv:2602.05425): generated by `R^z(θ) = e^{iθZ/2}`
  and `R^{xx}(θ) = e^{iθX⊗X/2}`.
* **Brod** (arXiv:1207.2126): matchgates include *all single-qubit
  `Z`-rotations*; they become universal when supplemented by `H`.
* **Jozsa, Miyake**: arbitrary single-qubit gates are simulable **on the first
  line only**; deeper linear terms "generally act across all the first `k` lines".
* **arXiv:2410.10068**, Theorem 6: the enlarged `(2n+1)` covariance matrix covers
  "**quadratic and linear** products of Jordan–Wigner Majoranas".

## Repair

Replace the brickwork by **`RXX`/`RZ`**:

* `RXX(θ) = exp(−iθ X_lX_{l+1}/2)` — quadratic, the genuine nearest-neighbour
  matchgate entangler;
* `RZ(θ) = exp(−iθ Z_l/2)` — quadratic, on every line.

Both act on Majoranas by Givens rotations, so the `O(n²d)` covariance evolution
is exact with no enlargement.  The benchmark keeps its intent: blind to
single-qubit `Z` error (the `RZ` layer commutes with the noise model) and
sensitive to the two-qubit entangling error.

If `RX` must stay, it is only exactly simulable **on the first line**, via the
`(2n+1)`-enlarged covariance matrix.  A single final `RX` layer used for readout
only is also fine, because a non-Gaussian layer can be absorbed into the
measurement rather than the state preparation.

## Relation to the other modules

`fhb-spectral` proves the spectrum `n − 2·|S|` of the walk Hamiltonian
`H = Σ_j X_j`.  That is a statement about the **generator**, not about
simulability, and it is unaffected by this note.
